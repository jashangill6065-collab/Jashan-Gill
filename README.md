# CrimeScene: Interactive Murder Mystery Game for Android 🔍🔪

Turn your home or backyard into an interactive crime scene. Each guest gets a secret character role, hidden motives, and objectives to investigate and solve a fake murder over 3–4 hours.

Built with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose (Material 3)**, and Android audio & graphic synthesis.

---

## 📖 The Murder Mystery: *The Poisoned Arbor & The Will of Alistair Vance*

* **The Victim:** **Lord Alistair Vance (Age 64)**, eccentric botanical tycoon found slumped across the stone birdbath at the garden gazebo.
* **Cause of Death:** Concentrated **Aconite ("Monkshood") Neurotoxin** slipped into his 1982 Port decanter, with staged post-mortem blunt head trauma via bronze fire poker.
* **The Murderer:** **Dr. Julian Ward**, Alistair's personal physician, who embezzled $3.2M from the Vance Medical Trust and faced immediate police arrest.
* **The 3 Major Plot Twists:**
  1. *The Double Poisoning:* Vivienne Delacroix brought arsenic powder in her makeup compact to poison Alistair's tea, but Dr. Ward's aconite struck first!
  2. *The Staged Weapon:* The bloody head wound was inflicted post-mortem to disguise aconite convulsions as an accidental fall.
  3. *The Dying Message:* Alistair Vance wrote a secret dying clue on his handkerchief in invisible botanical ink: `"J.W. ... Port ... Check Shed Safe"`.

## ✨ Features

* **🎭 Confidential Guest Dossiers:** Individual 4-digit passcodes protect private motives, alibis, and interactive party objectives with privacy shields.
* **🕵️ Playable Suspect Interrogation:** Confront suspects with tough questions to expose nervous body language tells and alibi discrepancies.
* **🔦 365nm UV Blacklight Scanner:** Toggle ultraviolet light on evidence to illuminate glowing fingerprints and hidden messages.
* **🧪 Forensic Chemistry Lab:** Simulated reagent titration tests with live progress indicators to differentiate Aconite and Arsenic.
* **📌 Interactive Murder Board:** Connect suspects, motives, and clues with red strings. Star your **⭐ Prime Suspect**, cross off suspects with **❌ Rule Out**, and write observations in your **Detective Notebook**.
* **🎛️ Host Game Master Console:** Party timer for all 5 acts, an interactive **Estate Blackout trigger** with thunder and lightning cues, soundboard, and physical home/backyard staging blueprints.
* **🎨 Graphics & Display Adjust Panel:** 4 visual theme filters (*Midnight Noir*, *Classic Film Noir B&W*, *Vintage Sepia*, *OLED High-Contrast*), ambient brightness slider (70%–130%), and cinematic vignette shadows.

## 🛠️ How to Build & Run in Android Studio

1. **Clone this repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone <YOUR_GITHUB_REPO_URL>
   cd CrimeScene
   ```
2. **Open in Android Studio:**
   - Select **Open an Existing Project** and choose the cloned folder.
   - Wait for Gradle to sync dependencies.
3. **Build and Run:**
   - Connect an Android device (Android 7.0 / API 24+) or launch an emulator.
   - Click the green **Run (Play)** button or execute:
     ```bash
     gradle assembleDebug
     ```
   - The generated APK will be at:
     `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`
